Retainer Privacy

Your account

You sign in with Google or with a link sent to your email. Retainer keeps your email address and, if you use Google, the name and picture Google shares with it. It asks Google for nothing else and cannot read your mail, files or contacts.

Your notes

What you capture (screenshots, what you say about them, and where they came from) is stored on your Mac. Speech is turned into text on your Mac.

If you turn on Cloud mode, your notes and their pictures are also stored in your account, on servers run by Supabase in the United States, so that they are the same on every Mac you sign in on. Only your account can read them. Turn Cloud mode off and nothing more is sent.

If you add an Anthropic key for search by meaning, the words of your notes are sent to Anthropic when you search.

What isn’t done

No advertising, no analytics, no selling or sharing of your data.

Deleting

To have your account and everything stored in it deleted, write to vincentzhou04@gmail.com.
